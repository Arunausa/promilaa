# Brand Guidelines

Project: Promilaa

Version: 1.0

---

# Brand Identity

Promilaa is a premium Bangladeshi women's fashion brand.

The brand specializes in:

- Kurti
- One Piece
- Two Piece
- Three Piece

Promilaa is NOT:

- A marketplace
- A discount store
- A fast-fashion platform
- A multi-vendor website

The brand should always feel elegant, modern, premium, trustworthy, and feminine.

---

# Brand Mission

Provide premium-quality women's ethnic fashion with a seamless online shopping experience for customers across Bangladesh.

---

# Brand Vision

Become one of Bangladesh's most trusted online women's fashion brands through quality products, premium presentation, and outstanding customer experience.

---

# Brand Personality

Always:

- Premium
- Elegant
- Minimal
- Feminine
- Modern
- Warm
- Trustworthy
- Clean

Never:

- Cheap
- Flashy
- Cluttered
- Overly colorful
- Marketplace-like
- Spammy

---

# Target Audience

Gender

Women

Age

16–45

Country

Bangladesh

Lifestyle

- Students
- Working Professionals
- Homemakers
- Fashion-conscious women

---

# Tone of Voice

Friendly

Simple

Professional

Confident

Respectful

Avoid:

- Aggressive sales language
- Clickbait
- Excessive emojis
- Fake urgency

---

# Visual Direction

Large whitespace

Premium photography

Editorial layouts

Minimal UI

Soft shadows

Rounded corners

Smooth animations

Elegant typography

---

# Color Palette

Primary

Black

White

Cream

Beige

Secondary

Soft Pink

Light Gold

Warm Gray

Accent

Deep Maroon

Use colors sparingly.

---

# Typography

Modern sans-serif fonts.

Prefer:

- Inter
- Plus Jakarta Sans
- Manrope

Readable font sizes.

Consistent spacing.

---

# Photography

High-resolution only.

Use:

- Studio shots
- Lifestyle shots
- Clean backgrounds
- Natural lighting

Avoid:

- Pixelated images
- Busy backgrounds
- Watermarks

---

# Product Presentation

Each product must include:

- Multiple Images
- Fabric Details
- Size Guide
- Care Instructions
- Color Options
- Accurate Pricing

---

# Homepage Feel

The homepage should communicate:

- Premium quality
- Seasonal collections
- New arrivals
- Trust
- Simplicity

Avoid overcrowding.

---

# Banner Style

Minimal text.

Strong visuals.

Clear CTA.

Seasonal promotions only.

---

# Product Cards

Show:

- Image
- Name
- Price
- Discount (if any)
- Wishlist
- Stock Badge

---

# Icons

Use simple outline icons.

Keep icon style consistent across the website.

---

# Animations

Use Framer Motion.

Animations should be:

- Smooth
- Fast
- Subtle

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Avoid excessive motion.

---

# Mobile Experience

Design mobile-first.

Large tap targets.

Fast loading.

Sticky bottom actions where appropriate.

---

# Trust Signals

Display:

- Secure Checkout
- Easy Exchange Policy (if applicable)
- Fast Delivery
- Customer Reviews
- Verified Payment Methods

---

# Social Media

Maintain consistent branding across:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok (Future)

Use the same colors, typography, and photography style.

---

# Brand Rules

Every design, feature, and marketing decision should reinforce:

- Premium quality
- Trust
- Simplicity
- Modern aesthetics
- Excellent customer experience

Never sacrifice brand consistency for short-term trends.
