Project: Promilaa

# Overview

Promilaa is a premium Bangladeshi women's fashion brand.

Version 1 focuses only on:

- Kurti
- One Piece
- Two Piece
- Three Piece

Every product must be carefully curated.

Quality is more important than quantity.

# AI Agent Rules
